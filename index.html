Welcome to Tennessee

Tennessee is a state with a little bit of everything, from the Great Smoky Mountains to busy cities like Nashville, Memphis, and Knoxville. It is well known for its music, history, beautiful scenery, and unique culture. Each part of the state has its own personality, which makes Tennessee interesting to learn about and explore. This website provides an overview of Tennessee and highlights three of its major cities: Nashville, Memphis, and Knoxville. Each city has its own history, population, economy, and characteristics that help show how different areas of Tennessee compare with one another.

Learn more about Tennessee at TN.gov.

Quick facts

  • Capital: Nashville
  • Nickname: The Volunteer State
  • Region: Southeastern United States
  • Known for: Music, history, and mountains
